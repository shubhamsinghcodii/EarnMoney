Good to know

Questions, answered.

Everything important before your first run.

+ Is TapWings free to play?

Yes. TapWings is free to play and supported by optional ads. There are no upfront charges.

+ How do withdrawals work?

The first request is ₹10. Later tiers are ₹5,000, ₹12,000 and ₹19,000. Every request is reviewed manually, and payout times are estimates, not guarantees. A 5-star rating may place a request in a priority review window of about 10 minutes; other requests are estimated within 3 working days.

+ Do I need an account to play?

No. TapWings uses a silent Firebase anonymous ID. Your name and UPI or PayPal details are only needed when you submit a payout request.

+ Which Android versions are supported?

TapWings requires Android 6.0 (API 23) or newer.

+ Is TapWings on the Play Store or App Store?

Not yet. Play Store and App Store releases are coming soon. A preview Android APK is available on this page.

+ How does ad consent work?

TapWings uses Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform. Where required, a consent form appears for personalized advertising. You can change or withdraw your choice from the app’s Wallet page and the Privacy & Ad Consent button.
